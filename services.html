What We Do

Our Services

From a single sadya hall to a full concert stage — here's everything we plan, start to finish.

💍

Weddings

Traditional Kerala weddings, Christian and interfaith ceremonies, and modern receptions — we plan every ritual and reception detail so your family can simply be present. Typically included: mandapam and stage décor, sadya and catering coordination, photography and videography liaison, guest logistics, and a dedicated day-of coordinator.

🏢

Corporate Events

Product launches, annual conferences, offsites and team celebrations, run to the minute so your leadership team never has to think about logistics. Typically included: venue sourcing, stage and AV setup, branding and signage, guest registration, and run-of-show management.

🎉

Private Parties & Birthdays

Milestone birthdays, anniversaries, baby showers and family get-togethers, styled to feel personal rather than generic. Typically included: theme-based décor, catering coordination, entertainment bookings, and venue setup and teardown.

🌴

Destination Events

Backwater resorts, hill station retreats and beachside venues across Kerala, planned for guests travelling in from outside the state. Typically included: venue shortlisting and site visits, guest travel and stay coordination, multi-day itinerary planning, and on-ground vendor management.

🎤

Stage Shows & Concerts

Live concerts, cultural programs and stage shows that need serious sound and lighting behind the scenes. Typically included: stage and truss setup, professional sound and lighting, artist and crew coordination, and crowd and security logistics.

🏬

Exhibitions & Trade Shows

Trade fairs, product exhibitions and expo stalls that need to stand out on a busy floor. Typically included: stall design and fabrication, branding and signage, staffing coordination, and setup and breakdown within venue timelines.
