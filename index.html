About Us

Rooted in Kerala. Built for every occasion.

Kerala Events Co. started with a single wedding in a backwater village hall and grew into a full-service team trusted for weddings, corporate launches, and private celebrations across the state. We handle the details — décor, catering coordination, sound, stage, logistics — so families and companies get to simply show up and celebrate.

  • 200+ events planned across Kerala since we started.
  • One point of contact from first call to final send-off.
  • Vendors we know — caterers, decorators, sound crews we've worked with for years.
